About Me

I am a business professional, student, and lifelong learner with a passion for the intersection of coffee, food, sustainability, and entrepreneurship. After more than six years with Starbucks, I have grown from a Barista into a Coffeehouse Leader, developing experience in people leadership, operations, talent development, customer experience, and business performance. My career in coffee has shaped not only my professional skills but also my belief that businesses can be both financially successful and meaningful members of their communities.

I am currently pursuing a B.A. in Business with a concentration in Food Industry Management and a minor in Sustainable Food Systems at Arizona State University's W. P. Carey School of Business. I am also a member of Barrett, The Honors College and Beta Gamma Sigma, and have developed leadership experience through my work as President of The Forge and involvement in student and professional organizations.

My academic and professional interests have expanded beyond traditional business management into digital marketing, data analytics, global business, and sustainable food systems. Through the Global Career Accelerator, internships, and hands-on projects, I have built experience with marketing strategy, social media management, KPI analysis, Excel, data visualization, and business research.

Looking ahead, I hope to combine my experience in coffee and business with my passion for sustainability and community. My long-term goal is to build an entrepreneurial venture in the coffee industry that reflects the values I have developed throughout my career: strong leadership, meaningful connections, responsible business practices, and a genuine desire to create something that serves people well.
